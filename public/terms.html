Terms & privacy

enrage.io is a free browser game. By playing it, you agree to the following.

The game

It's provided as is, with no warranty, and may change or go offline at any time. Don't automate it, overload it, or try to break it: requests are rate-limited, and abuse may be blocked.

Your voice

Only while you hold Space, your microphone streams to Deepgram, which turns it into text. The game doesn't store your audio.

What you say

The text goes to TypeSafe AI's Jev, which judges how Gorran takes it, and is logged with LangSmith so the judge can be improved. Don't say anything personal.

Everything else

There are no accounts, ads or cookies. The server sees your IP address, held in memory only, to rate-limit requests. The game is hosted on Railway and its font comes from Google Fonts, each under its own privacy policy.
