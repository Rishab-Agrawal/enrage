enrage is provided free of charge and as is, without warranty of any kind. It may be changed, suspended or withdrawn at any time. Automated access, excessive requests and attempts to disrupt the service are prohibited; requests are rate-limited, and abusive traffic may be blocked.

Microphone

Audio is captured only while the push-to-talk key is held, and is streamed to Deepgram for transcription. enrage neither records nor stores it.

Transcripts

Transcribed speech is sent to TypeSafe AI to determine the game's response, and logged in LangSmith to evaluate and improve that judgement. Avoid sharing personal information while playing.

Other data

enrage uses no accounts, advertising or cookies. IP addresses are held in memory for rate limiting only, and are not stored. The site is hosted by Railway and its font is served by Google Fonts. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.
